# Primary-300 score and concern-resolution alignment (v3 inputs)

Status: complete, paired, post-hoc descriptive analysis. This recomputes the prior alignment using corrected v3 concern records; it does not replace either underlying experiment.

## Paired result

- Matched cohort: 300/300; paired analysis includes 284 papers with at least one model-labeled valid and repairable concern. The other 16 have no eligible proxy denominator and are excluded, not assigned zero.
- Mean holistic score change (C5−C0): -0.2230/5.
- Mean concern-resolution proxy change (C5−C0): +0.0559.
- Pearson r: 0.0706; Spearman rho: 0.0687, paper bootstrap 95% CI [-0.0564, 0.1897].
- Paper-level direction counts (score sign, resolution-proxy sign): {(-1, 0): 122, (-1, 1): 25, (0, 0): 81, (1, 0): 26, (1, 1): 10, (0, 1): 20}.
- Among 55 papers with a positive resolution proxy change, score changes were negative for 25, tied for 20, and positive for 10.

## Concern-level decomposition

The 1,745 extracted concerns include 717 classified as requiring a new experiment and 92 as a core-method/claim defect; neither class enters the paired proxy. The 873 eligible concern pairs transition as follows: {('unresolved', 'unresolved'): 684, ('unresolved', 'partially_resolved'): 54, ('unresolved', 'fully_resolved'): 21, ('fully_resolved', 'fully_resolved'): 43, ('partially_resolved', 'partially_resolved'): 71}. Thus the positive mean proxy comes from model-assigned unresolved-to-partial/full transitions; it is not a broad shift over every concern. Upgrade rates differ by action type: {'existing_evidence': {'eligible_concerns': 80, 'unresolved_to_resolved': 24, 'upgrade_rate': 0.3}, 'clarification': {'eligible_concerns': 539, 'unresolved_to_resolved': 39, 'upgrade_rate': 0.07235621521335807}, 'reanalysis': {'eligible_concerns': 254, 'unresolved_to_resolved': 12, 'upgrade_rate': 0.047244094488188976}}. Exact quote matching among eligible concerns: C0 147/196 and C5 399/428. Exact string match verifies provenance only, not semantic support or resolution.

## Evidence-based attribution

1. **The metrics target different constructs and are given different context.** The primary scoring prompt receives the title, frozen manuscript, and supplement, but not the original reviews; it asks for a holistic 1–5 recommendation. The resolution prompt receives the original reviews and asks the model to extract and label individual concerns against both anonymous supplements. This protocol mismatch is directly confirmed in the frozen code/prompts. The proxy also averages only concerns MiniMax calls valid and repairable using existing evidence, clarification, or reanalysis; it excludes concerns requiring a new experiment, core-method/claim defects, and other ineligible labels. A narrow response can change a local concern label while leaving the overall recommendation unchanged or lower.
2. **The observed per-paper alignment is weak.** The latest v3-input Spearman estimate is near zero and its paper bootstrap interval spans negative and positive associations. Of the papers whose resolution proxy increased, fewer than one fifth had a positive score change. This directly shows that score movement is not a usable stand-in for this proxy in this cohort; it does not reveal which underlying mechanism caused the mismatch.
3. **A generic supplementary-material penalty and a C5-specific penalty are both visible in the score proxy.** C1 placebo (-0.071), C2 clarification (-0.054), C3 reproducibility (-0.092), and C5 (-0.233) all score below C0 on average. The predeclared, prompt-token-matched C5−C1 contrast is -0.162 (95% CI [-0.207, -0.118]). This confirms that this M3 scoring setup rated targeted material lower than a neutral appendix beyond a simple length difference. The negative C5−C0 direction also appears in the existing Qwen3 and Mistral panels (-0.857 and -0.133, respectively); these are still AI score proxies and have no matched concern-resolution panels. The observed score decline is therefore not unique to the M3 call, but its human meaning is unknown. Twenty-five C5 appendices are empty and copy the baseline score by protocol; among the 275 nonempty patches the mean score delta is -0.2545, so empty patches do not account for the negative mean.
4. **The measurement protocols also differ in replication and scale.** Holistic score averages three model calls per condition. The resolution outcome comes from one paired call that extracts up to six concerns and assigns full/partial/unresolved labels. The latter has a restricted denominator and more aggregation ties, so differences in stochastic noise, prompt framing, and scale may contribute.
5. **Evidence matching is not evidence sufficiency, and the proxy has a possible same-family leniency risk.** Exact quote checks establish lexical provenance only. They do not establish that the quote entails the response, is relevant to the concern, or supplies missing results. Among eligible concerns, no C5 item is labeled worse than C0; 75 move from unresolved to partial/full. That could reflect legitimate added support, but it could also reflect the same-model paired judge's preference or asymmetric decision boundary. Two quotes exceed the output word limit overall. None of these checks establishes expert-confirmed resolution.
6. **Remaining patch-level explanations are hypotheses.** The supplement may be too narrow, fail to address major concerns that need new experiments, introduce unsupported or weakly relevant claims, or draw attention to gaps. Judge-family style preferences and context effects may also matter. Existing aggregate data do not isolate these causes; expert-coded discordant cases are needed.

## Further exploratory checks

- **Actionability:** The proxy upgrade rate is highest when a concern is labeled addressable with existing evidence (24/80, 30.0%); it is lower for clarification (39/539, 7.2%) and reanalysis (12/254, 4.7%). This is consistent with evidence pointers helping most when the needed evidence already exists, but these action labels are model-generated.
- **Baseline-score strata:** C5−C0 averaged -0.124 for the low-baseline stratum (199 papers), -0.447 for the middle stratum (97), and -0.500 for the high stratum (4). The high stratum is too small for inference. The middle-stratum decline also appears against placebo; subgroup findings are exploratory and not a causal explanation.
- **Patch metadata:** Score deltas have descriptive Pearson correlations of -0.166 with appendix length, -0.175 with selected entry count, -0.185 with candidate source-quote match rate, and -0.445 with baseline score. The score-delta/baseline correlation is mathematically coupled because baseline is subtracted from the outcome; it is not evidence of baseline quality causing the decline. These are unadjusted associations; paper difficulty and number of concerns can confound patch metadata. They do not show that longer or better-matched patches lower scores.
- **Repeat variation:** Mean within-paper standard deviation across three M3 calls was 0.252 for C0 and 0.222 for C5. Repeated scoring reduces noise in the paper mean but does not address the missing review context or the one-call resolution proxy.
- **Score-rationale screen:** A literal keyword scan of the three-call weakness text found explicit “no/insufficient evidence” wording in 19 C0 papers, 29 C5 papers, and 241 placebo papers. “Unsupported/overclaim” terms appeared in 69 C0 versus 43 C5 papers. This crude lexical screen does not semantically code the rationale; it gives no evidence that unsupported claims broadly increased under C5 and cannot explain the numerical score shift.

## Interpretation

This analysis supports reporting reviewer-score change and concern-resolution proxy as separate outcomes. It does not establish expert-confirmed concern resolution or attribute the negative score change to a specific patch defect. The next attribution study should have blinded experts label concern validity, evidence entailment/relevance, resolution, and paper score separately; stratify by concern type and actionability; then audit discordant cases (proxy up/score down and proxy flat/score up) with reason codes. Preserve an untouched held-out subset for confirmatory evaluation.
